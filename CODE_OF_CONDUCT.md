# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not tolerated in issues, pull
requests or discussions. Assume good faith, be specific, and keep feedback about the work.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may restrict participation. Report problems
privately through the repository's security advisory form or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub.

This is a short statement in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
